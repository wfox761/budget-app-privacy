Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-11-08

This describes how the Budget App handles your information. It is a private application run by one person (the "owner") on the owner's own computer for the owner and a few family members. It is not offered to the public.

What is collected

  • Your sign-in: an email address and a password (stored only as a salted hash, never the password itself), and, if you turn it on, an authenticator-app second step.
  • Your budget: accounts, transactions, categories, bills and plans that you or the owner enter or import.
  • If a bank is connected through Plaid: the accounts you choose to share, their balances and their transactions. Your bank username and password are typed into Plaid's window and are never seen or stored by this app.

How bank connections work

Banks are connected through Plaid (plaid.com). Only the owner can start a bank connection, and only for accounts whose holder has agreed to it. Plaid's own privacy policy applies to what Plaid collects: https://plaid.com/legal/ You can ask to have a bank connection removed at any time (see Deletion).

Where it is kept and who can see it

  • Everything is stored on one computer controlled by the owner, in an encrypted database. Bank access tokens are encrypted separately, with a key kept apart from the database.
  • The computer is reachable only through a private VPN. No cloud provider holds your data.
  • The owner can see your plans. Other family members see only what the owner shares with them, and only with the role the owner chose (full, management or view only).
  • Encrypted backup copies are kept, including in a cloud-synced folder. They are unreadable without the encryption key, which is never copied there.
  • Your data is never sold, shared for advertising, or given to anyone else, except to Plaid as needed to fetch your bank data, or when the law requires.

How long it is kept

Until you or the owner delete it. Backup copies are replaced on a rolling schedule, so a deleted item can remain in old backups for a limited time.

Your choices (access and deletion)

Ask the owner (contact below) at any time to:

  • show you everything held about you,
  • remove a connected bank (this also tells Plaid to drop the connection),
  • delete your plans and your sign-in.

Changes

If this policy changes, the owner will tell everyone who uses the app before the change applies.
